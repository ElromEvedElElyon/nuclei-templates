## 2. TRIANGULAR SALES MODEL — How It Works Legally

### Definition (Venda Triangular / Operacao Triangular)
```
[Customer] <--- Invoice --- [Padrao Bitcoin (intermediary)]
     ^                              |
     |                              v
     +---- Direct shipment --- [Manufacturer/Supplier in China]
```

1. Customer buys on Mercado Livre from Padrao Bitcoin's store
2. Padrao Bitcoin invoices the customer (NF-e de servico or NF-e de venda)
3. Padrao Bitcoin sends the order to the Chinese supplier
4. Supplier ships directly via ML's China fulfillment hub
5. ML handles last-mile delivery in Brazil
6. Padrao Bitcoin receives payment from ML minus commissions

### Legal Basis
- **CNAE 7490104**: "Atividades de intermediacao e agenciamento de servicos e negocios em geral" — this is EXACTLY what triangular sales is
- **Simples Nacional**: Taxed under Annex V (or Annex III with Fator R optimization)
- **Dropshipping from China on ML**: ML requires products to be in Brazil OR shipped via their official cross-border program. The China hub solves this.

### CRITICAL WARNING: ML Dropshipping Rules
ML does NOT allow traditional dropshipping from China with long delivery times. However:
- Products shipped via ML's official China fulfillment hub ARE allowed
- Products must have tracking and meet ML's delivery SLA (up to 30 days for cross-border)
- Seller must have CNPJ and issue NF

### Alternative Model: Import + ML Full (Higher Margin, Higher Risk)
1. Import bulk from China (e.g., 1000 books)
2. Store in ML Fulfillment (Full) warehouse in Brazil
3. ML handles storage, picking, packing, shipping
4. Products get "Full" badge = higher conversion, faster delivery
5. **Required for maximum seller reputation and Buy Box wins**

### Recommended Approach: HYBRID
- **Phase 1** (Week 1-4): Cross-border via ML China hub (zero inventory risk)
- **Phase 2** (Month 2-3): Import best-sellers to ML Full (higher margins, faster delivery)
- **Phase 3** (Month 3+): Scale with ML Full + multi-product catalog

## 7. TAX & FISCAL — Simples Nacional Implications

### Company Tax Profile
- **Regime**: Simples Nacional
- **Porte**: EPP (faturamento ate R$4.8M/ano)
- **CNAE Principal**: 7490104 (intermediacao)
- **Annex**: V (default) or III (with Fator R optimization)

### Tax Rates by Annex (Simples Nacional)
**Annex V (Services — default for 7490104)**:
| Revenue Band (12 months) | Nominal Rate | Effective Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Up to R$180,000 | 15.50% | 15.50% |
| R$180K-$360K | 18.00% | ~16.5% |
| R$360K-$720K | 19.50% | ~17.5% |
| R$720K-$1.8M | 20.50% | ~18.5% |

**Annex III (with Fator R >= 28%)**:
| Revenue Band (12 months) | Nominal Rate | Effective Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Up to R$180,000 | 6.00% | 6.00% |
| R$180K-$360K | 11.20% | ~8.5% |
| R$360K-$720K | 13.50% | ~10% |
| R$720K-$1.8M | 16.00% | ~12% |

### Fator R Optimization
Fator R = Payroll (last 12 months) / Revenue (last 12 months)
- If Fator R >= 28%, company is taxed in Annex III (MUCH lower rates)
- **Strategy**: Ensure payroll (pro-labore + employees) is at least 28% of revenue
- Example: R$10,000/month revenue -> R$2,800+/month in payroll = Annex III

### Book Sales: Special Tax Treatment
1. **Import of books**: ZERO tax (constitutional immunity Art. 150, VI, "d")
2. **Sale of books on ML**:
   - **ICMS**: EXEMPT (constitutional immunity extends to sales)
   - **PIS/COFINS**: 0% rate on book revenue
   - **ISS**: Not applicable (sale of goods, not services)
   - **Simples Nacional**: Still applies to overall revenue, BUT book revenue may qualify for reduced rates
3. **Digital books (e-books)**: Same immunity (STF ruling RE 330.817)

### Intermediation (Triangular Sales) Tax
- Taxed as SERVICE under CNAE 7490104
- Revenue = commission/markup on the sale (NOT the full product price, if structured correctly)
- Example: Product sells for R$100, supplier cost R$40 -> taxable revenue = R$60 (margin)
- **Consult accountant** to structure as intermediation vs. resale for tax optimization

### ML Commission as Deductible Expense
- ML commissions (11-19%) are deductible business expenses
- Shipping costs are deductible
- Advertising costs (Mercado Ads) are deductible

### Monthly Tax Obligations (DAS — Simples Nacional)
- **Due date**: 20th of following month
- **Generated via**: PGDAS-D (portal do Simples Nacional)
- **Includes**: IRPJ, CSLL, PIS, COFINS, ISS (all in one payment)
